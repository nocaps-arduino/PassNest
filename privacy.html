PassNest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

PassNest stores your passes, uploaded files, and wallet data in your browser storage on the device you use. This prototype does not send your pass data to a server.

Data You Save

You may save provider names, booking references, dates, seat numbers, notes, barcode values, and uploaded boarding pass files. This data remains local unless you export it or share your device/browser profile.

Backups

The export feature downloads a JSON backup file to your device. Keep that file private because it may contain sensitive travel or ticket information.

Future Cloud Features

If account sync or cloud storage is added later, this policy should be updated before launch to explain hosting, retention, security, and deletion rights.
